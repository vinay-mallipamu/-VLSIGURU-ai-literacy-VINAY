## 1. What did I learn?

I learned that deep learning depends strongly on the quality of its data. Images are converted into numerical pixel values, prepared and labelled before they are used for training. I also learned the difference between training, validation and test data, and why the test data must remain separate during model development.

## 2. What was the most useful concept?

The neural-network learning cycle was useful to understand. The model first makes a prediction in the forward pass, calculates a loss, uses backpropagation to calculate gradients, and updates weights and biases through an optimiser.
