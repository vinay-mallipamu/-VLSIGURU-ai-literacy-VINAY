1)AI: The broad idea of making machines do things that normally need human intelligence, like planning, recognising or deciding. It includes old rule-based systems too. Example: a chess program that searches moves using hand-written rules and scoring.

2)Machine Learning: A part of AI where the system learns patterns from data instead of being given every rule. More examples usually mean better performance. Example: a spam filter trained on thousands of emails labelled spam or not spam.

3)Deep Learning: A part of ML that uses neural networks with many layers. It works well on messy data like images, speech and text, because it can learn useful features by itself. Example: face unlock on a phone recognising your face.

4)Generative AI: A part of deep learning where the model produces new content, rather than only labelling or predicting. Example: ChatGPT or Claude writing an email, or an image model drawing a picture from a text prompt.
