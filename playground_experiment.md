# Mission 5 – TensorFlow Playground Experiment

## Activity link

Open [TensorFlow Neural Network Playground](https://playground.tensorflow.org/).

## Steps to perform

1. Open the Playground in a browser.
2. Select a dataset and note which dataset you selected.
3. Record the selected features, number of hidden layers, neurons per layer, activation function, learning rate and regularisation setting (if used).
4. Start training and observe how the decision boundary changes.
5. Change one setting at a time and train again. This makes it easier to understand which setting affected the result.
6. Capture screenshots of the initial configuration and the trained result for your submission.

## Questions to answer from your experiment

1. Did the decision boundary separate the two classes clearly?
2. Did the model need more training to improve the boundary?
3. What happened when you changed the number of hidden layers or neurons?
4. Did you observe signs of overfitting, such as a complicated boundary that did not match the overall data pattern?
5. Which configuration worked better in your experiment, and what evidence supports your answer?
