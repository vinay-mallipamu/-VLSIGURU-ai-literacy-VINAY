5:Ask an AI a question you can verify, and check whether it was right

## 1. Prompt

What does Moore’s Law say, and when was it introduced?

## 2. AI Answer

The AI explained that Gordon Moore introduced Moore’s Law in 1965. He observed that the number of components on an integrated circuit was doubling every year. In 1975, he revised the prediction to approximately every two years.

## 3. Verification Sources

1. Linda Hall Library: https://www.lindahall.org/gordon-moore/
2. Semiconductor History Museum of Japan: https://www.shmj.or.jp/english/pdf/ic/exhibi721E.pdf

I used these sources to check whether the AI’s explanation matched the historical information.

## 4. Result

The answer was correct based on the sources I checked. They support the main facts about the year Moore introduced his observation and his later revision.

## 5. What My Test Proved and Did Not Prove

My test showed that the AI gave a correct answer to this particular question. However, checking one answer does not mean that every answer from AI will be correct. I would need to check other answers separately.

## 6. Lesson Learned

I learned that an answer can sound confident but still contain mistakes. It is better to verify important information using reliable sources before trusting or sharing it.
