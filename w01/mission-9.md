9:Write 5 rules for using AI responsibly

## 1. Check before trusting

Whenever AI gives me technical information, I will check it with a reliable source before using it. AI can make mistakes even when its answer sounds correct.

## 2. Keep private information safe

I will not share confidential information, company design files, RTL code or project details with public AI tools. I will use only information that is safe to share.

## 3. Take responsibility for my work

I will make sure I understand the answers and code I submit. Even if AI helps me, I am responsible for checking my work and correcting mistakes.

## 4. Try and test things myself

When I use AI to write code or explain a technical problem, I will try simple examples and check the results instead of blindly trusting the answer.

## 5. Use AI to improve my knowledge

I will use AI to learn new concepts, understand difficult topics and practise engineering problems. I will try to understand the solution and explain it in my own words instead of copying it directly.
