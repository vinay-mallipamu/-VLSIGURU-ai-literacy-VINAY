4: Ask an AI how an LLM works, then check its claims with reliable sources

## 1. My Explanation

An LLM (Large Language Model) is an AI model that generates text by predicting what comes next. When I enter a prompt, it reads the text and predicts the next token based on patterns it learned during training. A token can be a word, part of a word, or punctuation.

For example, if I type “The capital of India is”, the model may generate “New Delhi”. It continues predicting tokens until it completes the answer.

## 2. Claims I Checked

* **Claim 1:** LLMs predict the next token to generate text.
  **Result:** Confirmed using Google's Machine Learning Crash Course.

* **Claim 2:** Self-attention helps transformer models understand relationships between different tokens in the text.
  **Result:** Confirmed using the Google source and the original Transformer research paper.

## 5. One Correction or Clarification

The AI explained it as predicting the next word, but I learned that it actually predicts the next token. A token can be a complete word or just part of a word. I also understood that predicting tokens is the basic process, while chat assistants may receive additional training to improve their responses.
